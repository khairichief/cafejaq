Our story

Brewed with heart

How a love for good coffee and slow mornings became Cozy Cafe.

Where it began

A cozy corner for everyone

Cozy Cafe started with a simple idea: everyone deserves a warm, welcoming place to slow down.

We wanted a spot where students could study for hours, workers nearby could grab a quick lunch, and families could share a treat on the weekend — all under one roof. So we filled our space with comfy seats, soft lighting and the smell of fresh coffee and baking.

Today, Cozy Cafe is a little neighbourhood living room in Cadillac. Our bakers are in early to get the croissants and cinnamon rolls out of the oven, and our baristas know many regulars by name (and by order).
